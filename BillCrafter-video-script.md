# BillCrafter — Promo Video Script

**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover · **Language:** English · **Runtime:** ~80 seconds
**Placement:** YouTube (primary) + website hero
**Version:** 1.0 · Based on the live site at billcrafter.com

---

## Before you record — two things that decide whether this works

**1. The website hero plays muted.** Most visitors will see this film with no sound, so
every claim in the voiceover must also appear as on-screen text. The subtitle column
below is not optional decoration — it carries the whole message on the homepage.

**2. Record at desktop width.** The editor switches to a compact mobile layout below
560px. Record the browser at **1440×900** (export 1920×1080) so the line-item table,
side panel and document all show at full size.

## Shot list (record in this order — it's one continuous session)

1. **Cold open footage** — a competitor-style signup flow. Use a generic mockup you
   own, or shoot a plain "Create your account" form. Do not show a named competitor's
   branded UI.
2. **Fresh load** of billcrafter.com, blank invoice, cursor ready.
3. **Typing pass** — business, client, one line item, qty, rate. Type at a natural
   speed; do not speed-ramp this shot, the live-total reveal is the product.
4. **Style pass** — template style dropdown, two accent swatches, logo upload.
5. **Doc type pass** — Invoice → Estimate → Quote → Receipt.
6. **Download** — click Download PDF, capture the browser download, then the opened PDF
   with a 200% zoom on logo and totals.
7. **Account pass** — signup, invoice manager, saved clients, Duplicate.
8. **Hero hold** — finished invoice, static, 4 seconds of clean footage for the endcard.

## Accuracy checklist — do not drift from these

These are the claims the live site actually supports. Anything beyond them is a
liability, not a stronger ad.

- ✅ **1 free PDF export with no account**, then **3 per month** on a free account.
- ✅ **45 templates, 8 layouts, 12 accent colors.**
- ✅ **More than 25 currencies** (26 today, including USDT/USDC).
- ✅ Invoice, estimate, quote, receipt.
- ✅ PDF download and print on every plan.
- ❌ **Do not say or imply Pro can be bought.** Pro ($9.90/mo) is in *private beta* and
  is not on sale. Keep Pro-only features — Word/Excel export, PAID/UNPAID stamps, share
  links, recurring invoices — **out of this film entirely**. Showing them implies they're
  available.
- ❌ **No user counts, no "trusted by 10,000 businesses", no invented testimonials.**
  We have no such figure and inventing one is the fastest way to lose the trust the
  product is built on.
- ❌ Don't say "unlimited free invoices" — editing is unlimited, *exports* are not.
- ❌ Don't show a named competitor's real interface in the cold open.

## Production notes

**Pacing.** The two shots that sell this product are the **live totals updating** and
the **PDF zoom**. Give each a full beat. Everything else can be trimmed.

**Cursor.** Enable cursor highlighting and click rings — on YouTube at small sizes,
viewers lose the pointer otherwise.

**Music.** Light, minimal, percussive. Duck 6 dB under the voiceover. For the website
hero, deliver a **muted-safe cut** where the on-screen text alone carries the story.

**Grade.** Cold and gray for the 7-second cold open, then warm and bright from the cut
at 0:07 onward. That single contrast does the persuading — the voiceover shouldn't have
to insult anything.

**Captions.** Burn in open captions for the hero version; upload a proper `.srt` to
YouTube (auto-captions mangle "BillCrafter").

**Endcard.** Hold `billcrafter.com` for 3+ seconds. Add a YouTube end screen linking to
the site over the final 5 seconds if runtime allows.
